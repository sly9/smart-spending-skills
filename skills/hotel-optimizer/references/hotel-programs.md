# Hotel programs worth checking

Evaluate a program only when the traveler has the card or status, or when the saving is large enough to mention it. **Terms change often, and this file was written from memory of terms as of 2025–2026. Treat every mechanic, ratio, credit amount and benefit below as a pointer to verify on the live page, not as a fact.** Don't copy any number from this file into a Net Cost.

## Premium-card hotel programs

Booked through the card's own travel site, usually paid with that card. Benefits are on top of the rate. Many luxury and upscale chain hotels in these programs still credit brand points, elite nights and elite benefits; verify for the specific brand.

### Amex Fine Hotels + Resorts (FHR) and The Hotel Collection (THC)
- **Who:** Amex Platinum (and Business Platinum) for FHR and THC; some other Amex cards for THC.
- **FHR benefits** (typical): daily breakfast for two, a property credit of around $100, room upgrade when available, noon check-in when available, guaranteed 4 pm late checkout, Wi-Fi.
- **THC benefits** (typical): a property credit of around $100 and a room upgrade when available; a minimum stay of two nights.
- **Lodging credit** (checked on amex.com on 2026-11-26): the Platinum card's "$600 Lodging Credit" gives up to $300 back each half-year (January–June, July–December) on prepaid ("Pay Now") FHR, THC and Airbnb Luxe bookings through Amex Travel. THC needs two nights or more; from 2027-01-01 FHR does too. The half-year is decided by when the charge is processed, not the stay date. How much is left shows in the card's benefits tracker, not on the program page.
- **Where to search:** americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/platinum-card-hotel-programs/ (destination, then dates). Its date picker can be hard to drive by automation; hand it off if needed.
- **Earn:** prepaid hotels through Amex Travel have earned an elevated rate on the Platinum card. Check.
- **Compare:** FHR rates are often the same as the hotel's flexible public rate, sometimes higher. Compare against the brand's member rate plus the traveler's elite benefits, not against the public rate alone.

### Chase The Edit
- **Who:** Chase Sapphire Reserve (and some other Sapphire cards).
- **Benefits** (typical): daily breakfast for two, a property credit of around $100, room upgrade when available, early check-in and late checkout when available, Wi-Fi.
- **Credit:** the Reserve has had a credit for prepaid The Edit bookings of two nights or more, split by half-year. In 2026 there has also been an extra credit for a short list of hotel brands booked through Chase Travel (shown as "Select hotels credit", expiring 2026-12-31). Check the current amounts, the minimum stay and what's left.
- **Where to look:** the Chase Travel home page (from ultimaterewardspoints.chase.com → Travel) shows a benefits bar with each credit's remaining amount, and the search results mark The Edit hotels. The results page renders without readable text, so use screenshots there. Many The Edit rates on Chase Travel are prepaid but refundable until a few days before arrival; check each one.
- **Earn:** Chase Travel bookings have earned an elevated rate on the Reserve. Points Boost has offered higher redemption value for points on selected hotels. Check both.

### Other programs
- **Capital One Premier Collection and Lifestyle Collection** (Venture X): similar benefits and a travel credit usable on Capital One Travel.
- **Citi, U.S. Bank, Bilt and others** have their own hotel collections or credits. Check only if the traveler has the card.

## Major hotel programs

### Marriott Bonvoy
- **Awards:** dynamic pricing. Stays of five award nights get one night free (the cheapest). **Resort fees are generally charged on award stays.**
- **Certificates:** co-brand cards give annual free-night certificates with a points cap. Some caps can be topped up with a limited number of points; check the current top-up limit. Certificates expire about a year after they're issued.
- **Elite:** Gold typically gets a points bonus, late checkout when available and an upgrade when available; breakfast starts at Platinum.
- **Transfers in:** Chase Ultimate Rewards and Amex Membership Rewards have transferred 1:1; Amex has run transfer bonuses to Marriott (35% in late 2026). Marriott points also transfer out to airlines.
- **Where to look:** certificates are under My Account → Awards (not Gift Cards, which sits right below it). Expired certificates may still be listed.
- **Cash + points** is available.

### Hilton Honors
- **Awards:** dynamic pricing. Members with status get the fifth night free on standard room rewards. **Resort fees are waived on award stays.**
- **Certificates:** some Hilton co-brand cards give free-night certificates, usable at most properties.
- **Elite:** Gold typically gets a daily food-and-beverage credit in the U.S. (breakfast elsewhere), a points bonus and an upgrade when available.
- **Transfers in:** Amex Membership Rewards has transferred 1:2. Hilton points are usually worth well under a cent each; check the valuation.
- **Points & Money** is available.

### World of Hyatt
- **Awards:** award charts by category with off-peak, standard and peak prices. **No resort fees on award stays.** Hyatt points tend to get the best value of the major programs.
- **Certificates:** the co-brand card has given an annual Category 1–4 certificate, plus another one after a spending threshold. No points top-up.
- **Elite:** Discoverist is the entry tier (late checkout when available, upgrades at some properties). Breakfast starts at Globalist.
- **Transfers in:** Chase Ultimate Rewards has transferred 1:1, but TPG reported in November 2026 that it is now 4:3 for some cardholders. Read the ratio on the traveler's own Chase transfer page.
- **Booking pages:** the cheapest "Members Save More" rate can be prepaid and non-refundable. Over holidays some hotels require cancellation weeks ahead (21 days at one Kyoto hotel over New Year). The account's free-night certificates are at /profile/en-US/awards.
- **Small Luxury Hotels (SLH)** and some other partner hotels can be booked with Hyatt points.

### IHG One Rewards
- **Awards:** dynamic pricing. IHG co-brand cardholders have got the fourth award night free.
- **Certificates:** the co-brand card gives an annual certificate with a points cap; top-up has been allowed.
- **Transfers in:** Chase Ultimate Rewards has transferred 1:1, but IHG points are usually worth little. Check the valuation before transferring.

### Others
Accor, Wyndham, Choice and Best Western are transfer partners of some programs (for example Capital One, Citi, Bilt). Check only when they are on the shortlist.

## Japan
- Room rates usually exclude a service charge (around 13–15%) and 10% consumption tax, together roughly 24% on top of the rate. Cities such as Kyoto and Tokyo add a per-person, per-night accommodation tax, tiered by rate and often collected at the hotel rather than in the online total. Award nights may still owe it.

## Discount rates
- **AAA:** usually 5–15% off at the major brands, often refundable. Bring the card.
- **Corporate and employer rates:** some employers have negotiated rates or a travel portal. A personal-trip booking must follow the employer's rules.
- **Senior, government, military:** need proof at check-in.
- **Warehouse club (Costco Travel):** resort and package deals, sometimes with a Costco gift card. Check whether the brand credits points and elite benefits for these bookings.
- **Brand packages:** breakfast, parking or credit packages can beat the base rate plus paying separately.

## Online travel agencies and card travel portals
- **Agencies:** Expedia, Hotels.com, Booking.com and their loyalty programs. Cashback portals pay on some of them; check CashbackMonitor.
- **Card portals:** Chase Travel, Amex Travel (non-FHR/THC hotels), Capital One Travel. Elevated card earn, and sometimes points redemption at a better rate.
- **Downside:** they are usually third-party bookings. Brand points, elite night credit and elite benefits usually don't apply, the hotel may give the worst rooms, and changes go through the agency. Recommend them only when they are clearly cheaper and the traveler doesn't care about status benefits.

## Tools
- **Google Hotels:** market rates across channels, and price tracking by email.
- **Award search tools** such as Rooms.aero, MaxMyPoint and Awayz: find award availability across dates. Confirm on the brand's own site.
- **FHR and The Edit search tools** built by enthusiasts can find properties and dates faster than the card sites. Confirm on the card's own site.
- **Price-drop tracking** services re-check a refundable booking and alert when the rate drops. Confirm the new rate on the brand's site before rebooking.
