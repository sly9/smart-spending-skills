# Merchant-tied programs worth checking

Evaluate these only when the shopper has the card or membership, or when the saving is large enough to suggest getting it. **Terms change often. Treat every mechanic below as a pointer to verify on the live page, not as a fact.** Don't copy any rate or cap from this file into a Net Cost.

## B&H Photo
- **Payboo card:** rebates the sales tax at checkout (shown as an instant credit) on eligible B&H purchases. Check whether the card itself has Extended Warranty or Purchase Protection; store-style cards often don't. Compare the tax saved against the protection lost from a premium card.
- Employee and corporate discount links exist for some employers. They may not stack with portals.
- Portal rates for B&H are often low and come with category exclusions.

## Costco
- **Executive membership:** an annual reward on eligible purchases, with an annual cap. Check the current rate and cap.
- **Costco co-brand card:** extra rewards at Costco; it stacks with the Executive reward. Check its current benefit guide for warranty and purchase protection.
- **Returns:** generous for most items, with a shorter window for some electronics (TVs, computers, major appliances). Some electronics come with a free warranty extension or support service. Verify the current terms.
- **Price adjustment:** within a set window after purchase if the price drops. Verify the window.
- Costco.com and warehouse prices and offers can differ.

## Amazon
- **Co-brand card:** an elevated rate at Amazon that may require an active Prime membership. Check the card's current benefit guide for its protections before assuming another card protects better.
- Buy items shipped and sold by Amazon for clean returns and A-to-z coverage.
- Amazon generally doesn't refund price drops after purchase. Return windows are extended over the holidays.
- Most cashback portals don't cover Amazon.

## Best Buy
- Return windows depend on the membership tier. A price-match guarantee applies within the return window. Verify both.
- The store card may offer elevated rewards or financing instead of rewards.
- Open-box items are cheaper but may have different return terms and are often excluded from portals.

## Other common programs
- **Target store card:** a percentage off Target purchases.
- **Apple Card:** elevated rate at Apple. Apple Education and Apple Certified Refurbished are clean, authorized discounts.
- **Rotating 5% cards** (Chase Freedom, Discover it): quarterly categories that need activation and have a quarterly cap.
- **Choice-category cards** (for example Bank of America Customized Cash, U.S. Bank Cash+): the shopper may need to switch the category to online shopping or electronics before buying, and bank relationship tiers can boost the rate.
- **Online-retail bonus cards** (for example Amex Blue Cash Everyday): elevated rate on U.S. online retail, with an annual cap.
- **Premium travel cards** (for example Amex Platinum, Chase Sapphire Reserve): low earn on retail, but often the strongest Extended Warranty, Purchase Protection and Return Protection. Verify the current terms.
